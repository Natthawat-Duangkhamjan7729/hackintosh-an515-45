## Hardware/ACPI work

- Windows PnP identifies I2C touchpad ELAN050A and PS/2 keyboard FUJ7401, with AMD GPIO AMDI0030.
- DSDT exposes touchpad at `\_SB.I2CD.TPAD`, HID entry ELAN050A and compatible ID PNP0C50. VoodooI2CHID is the candidate satellite, not VoodooI2CELAN selected merely from the ELAN name.
- TPAD `_CRS` depends on OSYS >=0x07DC for I2C/GPIO resources. That warrants further OSYS/initialization analysis; no blanket XOSI rename was applied.
- VoodooPS2Controller 2.3.7 and VoodooI2C 2.9.1 RELEASE downloaded from their official release pages. Not injected yet. Avoid duplicate VoodooInput plugins when assembling the dependency graph.
- SSDT5 disassembled with DSDT and other SSDTs as external references. GPU resource is `\_SB.PCI0.GPP0.PG00`; its `_OFF` invokes the firmware's SGPC/GC6 paths. PEGP `_PS3` gates power-off on OPCE=3, so calling `_PS3` alone cannot be assumed to power down the GPU. No GPU power SSDT enabled yet.
- Actual laptop EC is `\_SB.PCI0.LPC0.EC0`; no EC, USBX, ALS0 or PNLF device found in inspected disassemblies. Added only a Darwin-visible fake EC/USBX source in `components/ACPI`, leaving the real EC intact. Power properties use Dortania's documented laptop defaults, not measured electrical limits.
- `SSDT-EC-USBX.dsl` compiled by iasl 20260408: 0 errors, 0 warnings, 0 remarks. No runtime validation yet.
- AMD Vanilla's four core-count replacements changed from zero to eight physical cores; all other upstream patch entries preserved. See `components/AMD-core-patch-diff.json`. No AMD patches are injected by the firmware diagnostic EFI.
